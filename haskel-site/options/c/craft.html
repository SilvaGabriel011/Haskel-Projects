Our story

Built on the jobs nobody else wanted

Haskel Projects is a small Adelaide stonemasonry business. It started with the calls other fabricators would not return — a single ensuite vanity, a benchtop for a bar, a chipped corner three weeks before a house went on the market.

Those jobs still make up most of our week. We are set up for single pieces: 20mm engineered and sintered stone, templated and cut to your room rather than to a production run.

Our values

Five things that decide every job

I

The small job matters

A single vanity top is a real job with a real deadline. We price it like one and we book it like one. No minimum slab, no being talked into a full kitchen.

II

You talk to the mason

No call centre, no sales rep, no subcontracted stranger. The person who quotes your job is the person who templates, cuts and fits it.

III

Repair before replace

If a chip can be filled and polished for a fraction of a new top, we will tell you — even though it is the smaller invoice.

IV

Nothing good gets binned

Offcuts from big slabs become vanities, hall tables and bar tops. Premium stone, a fraction of the price, and far less waste heading to landfill.

V

We leave it clean

Dust controlled, offcuts taken away with us, and your room usable by the time we drive off.
