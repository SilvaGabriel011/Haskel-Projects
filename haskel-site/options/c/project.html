Before you ask

Common questions

Do you take single-piece jobs?

Yes — that is most of what we do. One vanity top, one benchtop, one repair. There is no minimum slab and we will not push you toward a full kitchen you did not ask for.

Can a chip be repaired instead of replaced?

Very often. Send a photo and we will tell you honestly whether a fill and polish will hold, or whether the piece is past it. If a repair works, that is what we will quote.

What are offcuts, and why are they cheaper?

They are remnants left over from large slabs. Same stone, same finish, a fraction of the price — the limit is size, so they suit vanities, splashbacks, hall tables and bar tops. Ask what is on the rack this week.
