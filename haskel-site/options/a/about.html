Our mission

good stone, without the runaround

Most fabricators are set up for whole kitchens. Ring them about one vanity top or a chipped corner and you get a quote designed to make you go away.

We built Haskel Projects to take exactly those jobs. One mason, measuring, cutting, polishing and installing your piece — so the person who quotes you is the person who turns up.

Make good stone possible for the jobs the big shops will not take.

What we stand on

our values

Five things that decide how we quote, cut and finish every job.

I

The small job matters

A single vanity top is a real job with a real deadline. We price it like one and we book it like one.

II

You talk to the mason

No call centre, no sales rep. The person who quotes your job is the person who cuts and fits it.

III

Repair before replace

If a chip can be filled and polished for a fraction of a new top, we will tell you — even though it is the smaller invoice.

IV

Nothing good gets binned

Offcuts from big slabs become vanities, hall tables and bar tops. Premium stone, a fraction of the price, less waste.

V

We leave it clean

Silica dust is controlled, offcuts go with us, and your kitchen is usable when we drive away.
