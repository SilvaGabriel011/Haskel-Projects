What suits what

A quick guide before you pick a colour.

Kitchen benchtops

Heat and stain resistance matter most. Sintered stone and 20mm engineered both hold up; darker colours show dust and water marks more.

Bathroom vanities

Small footprint, so this is the best place to use an offcut. Almost any stone in the range works.

Laundry & pantry

Hard-wearing and forgiving. Concrete looks and mid-greys hide the daily mess best.

Furniture & TV units

No plumbing cut-outs, so veining and drama can lead. Calacatta and nero assoluto do the heavy lifting here.
