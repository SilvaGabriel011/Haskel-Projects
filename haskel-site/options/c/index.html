Our mission

Good stone should not be reserved for whole-kitchen budgets

Most fabricators are set up for full kitchens. Ring them about one vanity top, or a chipped corner, and the quote is designed to make you go away.

Haskel Projects exists for exactly those jobs. One mason measures, cuts, polishes and installs your piece — so the person who prices the work is the person who turns up to do it.
